# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Yuki Sakai ("the Developer") handles user information in the app "Past the Last Train" ("the App").

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any user information. No personally identifiable information, device information, usage data, or crash data is sent to the Developer or to any third party.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores your game progress and settings only on your device. This data is never sent off the device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services for analytics, advertising, or payments.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App does not collect user information, no information is shared with third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be changed without notice. Any changes take effect when posted on this page.
