# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Yuki Sakai ("the Developer") handles user information in the app "The Cat's Atelier" ("the App").

## 1. Information the Developer Collects

The Developer does not collect any user information through the App. The App has no account registration and no feature for entering personal information.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores your game progress and settings only on your device. This data is never sent to the Developer, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## 3. Advertising

The App shows video ads only when you choose to watch one to see a hint. Ads are served by Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC.

To serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob automatically collects the following information and sends it to Google:

- Device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) on iOS, and the advertising ID and App Set ID on Android)
- Your IP address and the approximate location estimated from it
- Interaction information, such as ad views, taps, and app launches
- Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and crashes

This information is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. The Developer receives only aggregated information from Google AdMob, such as the number of ad views, and does not receive any information that identifies you personally.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Consent and Changing Your Settings

- In regions where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the App shows a screen asking for your consent to the use of information for advertising when you start the game. You can change your choices at any time from "Privacy Settings" in the App's Options.
- On iOS, the App asks whether you allow tracking for advertising through App Tracking Transparency. If you do not allow it, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is not used. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings app.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

## 5. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the Developer does not collect user information, the Developer does not sell or provide it to third parties. Information for advertising is sent by Google AdMob, as described in Section 3.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be changed without notice. Any changes take effect when posted on this page.
